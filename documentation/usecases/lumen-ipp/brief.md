# Lumen Institutional Partner Program

**Status:** draft
**Owner:** Alex
**Anonymised:** yes, confirmed by Alex

## Challenge
How might we help librarians at our partner institutions recognize the value of their agreement with Lumen Scholarly Publishing and demonstrate it convincingly to the budget holders at their institution?

## Context
Through the Institutional Partner Program (IPP), universities and research institutions enter into partnerships with Lumen Scholarly Publishing. Affiliated authors receive discounts on publication fees (APCs); institutions can also cover these fees centrally, via collective invoicing or a prepayment account. Our contacts are usually librarians or data stewards. They rarely control the budget themselves, but they must justify the partnership internally to library or research leadership.

## Current situation
Partner institutions have access to a dashboard in our submission system, Manuscripta (submissions, status, APCs, discounts, CSV export). This dashboard is mainly an administrative and invoicing tool, not a way to make the partnership’s value visible. For renewal discussions, our IPP team partly assembles reports by hand, for example with Altmetric. Anecdotal evidence suggests that librarians are reluctant to log in to dashboards, prefer prepared reports delivered regularly, and above all need material to convince decision-makers at their own institution.

## Possible value
Possible kinds of value include savings from the partnership, the impact of publications (citations, Altmetric, mentions in policy documents), meeting open-access and open-data requirements, and the involvement of the institution’s own researchers as reviewers or editors. Which of these actually matter in a renewal decision, and by how much, is an open question.

## Goal
In the long term (two years), partner institutions should renew their agreement because evidence of its value reaches decision-makers without manual effort, and a growing share should expand the partnership.
